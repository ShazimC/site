*In the name of God, the All-Loving, the Ever-Loving.*\
*All praise and gratitude is to God,*\
*The most beautiful orchestrator of this reality.*

---

Rarely does one come across human writing that so beautifully points towards the signs of God. Not so long ago, I had the delight of finishing *Orb: On the Movements of the Earth*, a fictional story about those seeking the truth of God's cosmos in early 15th century dominantly Christian Europe.

In the very first words of the story, I found myself in movement as well.\
I pray nothing but divine guidance for the author as he continues in his *thaumazein*.

I will not narrate the story itself, I recommend all to experience it themselves, giving it the  attention and thought that it deserves.

Rather, I hope to simply be a part of the symphony it points to.

---

> *To obtain bread, one offers coin.*\
> *To obtain rights, one offers taxes.*\
> *To obtain wages, one offers labor.*\
> *So then, what must one offer up to obtain the truth?*

Has it not been man's honesty that has developed him through the years?\
Has it not been his humility to what he saw that succumbed him to say that he doesn't know?\
Has it not been the mountainous, fiery grit of the few that was rewarded?

And has it not been the All-Loving, that made the truth clear to the humble and deniers alike?\
Does the sun shine on only the good? and not on those who hide the truth?\
And He has not made it difficult to bask in.

Truly, there are truths man is born with, and those he can make an offer to obtain.\
A worthy offer.\
Complete surrender.\
To the symphony of the heavens and the earth,\
clear to all.

To bow to the majesty clear to the eye,\
that is an offer,\
worthy of its truth.

Sifting through the corruption of scriptures past, one can surely find stars in the night. And there are signs in them for those seeking with sincerity. Those who are not, no matter how they try, they can never take from the script of nature, nor inject their ugliness into the heavens.

So let the truth seekers turn to the stars, no matter which era under the moon they reside. Let them find clarity in the oneness of the cosmos, the system that never tires of singing His magnificence, through all countless lives of men.

Let them read His poetry and find Him, in peace.

---

> A life that isn't frightening is missing its essence.

Man does not like to feel fear. The avoidance of fear and pain are what many western philosophies use as their foundation. "What must we always do to reduce our feelings of fear and pain? We must create industries, factories, enormous empires dedicated to reducing pain and fear." But no matter what man seems to do, he just can't get rid of these two things from his life, and for those rich few who do manage to do this for their material world, can still never escape death and its anticipation.

Have we ever questioned whether that foundation that we built skyscrapers atop of, was rational to begin with? Are we ever going to truly rid ourselves of fear and pain? The obvious answer is no. But one may still argue "But we must still continue to reduce them as much as possible." to which I'd say yes let's but with clear understanding of what we're actually going to end up accomplishing. We are not going to rid the world of fear and pain, it's embedded into the foundation of how life operates on Earth. It's fear that drives the animals to survive and it's fear that drives all growth itself.

It's fear that wakes man up in the morning to strive for his family's well being.\
It's fear of failure or not accomplishing a dream that drives the student to toil in the text.\
It's fear for the life of one's child that drives the mother to lift unimaginable weights.

Or is it love?

Love, fear, and even hate. These are all interconnected entities that co-exist and depend upon each other within the hearts. Like different sides of the same coin. Or perhaps different sides of the same heart.

It's a mother's love for her child that drives her to be fearful of their future.\
It's a judge's love for justice that drives his hatred of injustice.\
And it can also be a hate for another people, that can drive those who share it to love each other above others. And drive them to be fearful of their hated ones coming to power.

An example may make this more concrete - the KKK hate black people. Those within and outside the cult that share this hate band together and support each other in suppressing black people when they think they are behaving in a way that causes the KKK to fear.

Another more recent example is conservative Americans banding together and supporting each other out of their shared hate & fear of Islam and Muslims.

Ok all this makes sense. Let's admit that there truly is no way to get rid of fear and hate just as much there may be no way to get rid of love, are we then to say that we should stop striving to reduce fear and pain from those suffering in this world? What of those in Gaza constantly being bombed and starved? Should we not rush to their aid?

There is quite possibly almost nothing else we should prioritize above that.

I've come to learn that fear and pain are not venomous enemies that we must wage war against every second of our lives. I've come to learn that fear and pain are neutral entities, tools, that when put in different contexts, take on different colors.

In the context of love, growth, and accomplishment, fear and pain take on one color and in the context of injustice and oppression, they take on another.

With or without conscious choice, fear is inseparably essential to life. Without conscientious choice however, that tool can be misapplied towards misalignment from God's designs.

And misalignment from God's design is ultimately what constitutes in destruction, damage, and injustice.

---

Rafael's battle of rationality. Is it more rational to prioritize self-preservation above everything or to be aligned with the rational beauty of the cosmos?

What is truly more rational? To prolong your life for a few more years through dishonesty or to prioritize honesty above everything even if it means your life ends sooner than you'd like it to? What's so great about honesty?
